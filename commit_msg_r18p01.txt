## Capabilities detection logic

3-фазный auto-detect по имени модели:

1. **Family defaults** → Reasoning=true
   - qwen3, qwen3.5, qwen3.6, deepseek-r1, kimi-k2, gemma-4
   - seed-oss, apriel, smallthinker, step-3.5

2. **Explicit non-reasoning** → Reasoning=false
   - содержит "instruct" или "-it"

3. **Explicit reasoning** → Reasoning=true
   - суффикс -thinking, -reasoning, -r1

Vision: llava, moondream, minicpm-v, qwen-vl, qwen2.5-vl, gemma3, pixtral
Tools: всё кроме encoder-only (bge, e5) и устаревших (gpt2, falcon)
